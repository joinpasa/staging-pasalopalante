About Pásalo Pa'lante

Pásalo Pa'lante is a global kindness movement created as a sister initiative of Te Amo PR, a nonprofit organization based in Puerto Rico. The phrase means "pass it forward," and the movement is built around a simple belief: kindness becomes more powerful when it is shared, repeated, and passed from person to person. While teamopr.org is the primary organizational website for Te Amo PR, pasalopalante.com is the dedicated movement platform for public participation, ambassadors, kindness campaigns, school and community activations, and global storytelling.

How it works

Pásalo Pa'lante begins with a simple act of kindness. A person helps, encourages, serves, gives, thanks, forgives, listens, supports, or uplifts someone else — then invites that person to pass kindness forward. The movement is simple enough for anyone to join and structured enough for schools, organizations, companies, municipalities, churches, and nonprofits to activate at scale. Read more at /how-it-works and /programs.

Get involved

Individuals, families, schools, nonprofits, companies, municipalities, ambassadors, and partners can all participate. Whether you do one act of kindness or help mobilize an entire community, your participation matters. Start at /get-involved or make a pledge at /commit.
